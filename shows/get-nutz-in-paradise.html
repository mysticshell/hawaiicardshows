The Story

GetNutz (@getnutzwearhawaii) started as a Hawai‘i streetwear and community brand — and threw its first card show as a small, homegrown first event. It clicked. So GetNutz teamed up with Paradise Cards (@paradisecards_) to build something bigger: Get Nutz in Paradise.

The result isn't just a card show — it's a card show, craft fair, and block party all at once, held at the Kroc Center in Ewa Beach. The August 2026 edition pulled in 5,000+ people and 140+ vendors across cards, crafts, and food, with live music all day. Cards are one part of a full family day out — which is exactly what makes it different.

FAQ

What is Get Nutz in Paradise?+
It's a card show, craft fair, and block party in one — a collaboration between GetNutz and Paradise Cards, held at the Kroc Center in Ewa Beach, O‘ahu. Alongside a full card-show floor you'll find craft vendors, food, and live music, so it's built for the whole family.
When and where is the next one?+
Get Nutz Christmas in Paradise is December 19, 2026 at the Kroc Center Hawaii (91-3257 Kualakai Pkwy, Ewa Beach). Times and the VIP package are being finalized — upcoming dates are always listed above.
How do I become a vendor?+
Vendor sign-ups and details are handled at getnutzinparadise.com, or reach out to @getnutzwearhawaii on Instagram.
Is there a VIP option?+
Yes — past editions have offered VIP early entry for the card-show floor, and a VIP package is planned for the Christmas edition. Check getnutzinparadise.com for the current package and pricing.
Is it family-friendly?+
Very. With crafts, food, and live music alongside the cards, it's designed as a full day out for families — not just collectors.
