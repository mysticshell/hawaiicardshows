The Challenge

Juan Guy's Gym Challenge is a traveling card show series on Maui, hosted by @juanguyscollection. Every show is branded after a Pokémon gym city — Pewter, then Cerulean, and next Vermillion — so the series plays out exactly like a trainer's journey across the region, one gym at a time.

What makes it different from any other trade show: at each stop you can earn a serialized, limited gym badge — a real, numbered collectible you can only get by showing up and taking on that city's Gym Leaders. It's a hobby event built like a game, and the badges are the reason collectors keep coming back for the next city. The destination? The same one every trainer chases — the Indigo League.

New to card shows? Read our beginner's guide. Coming from another island for it? See the Hawaii card-show trip planner.

How the Gym Badges Work

1

Serialized & limited

Each city's badge is numbered and produced in a limited run — once they're claimed, they're gone.

2

Earn them from Gym Leaders

Badges come from participating vendors — the "Gym Leaders." Each Leader sets their own challenge to earn theirs.

3

Validate your run

Once you've earned a badge, get it validated at the show to lock in your entry.

4

Collect & compete

The more badges you earn, the more entries you stack toward the grand prize.

$1,000
At the Vermillion City stop, collecting gym badges earns entries toward a $1,000 grand-prize shopping spree — winner drawn Saturday at 8 PM, or once all badges are claimed. Prizes and badge rules are set per event.

FAQ

What is Juan Guy's Gym Challenge?
It's a Maui card show series where each show is branded after a Pokémon gym city (Pewter, Cerulean, Vermillion…). At each stop you can earn a serialized, limited gym badge — a real numbered collectible — building the feel of a trainer's journey toward the Indigo League.
When and where is the next gym?
The Vermillion City Trade Show is Sept 25–26, 2026 at 1552 Mill Street, Wailuku — Friday 4–10 PM and Saturday 12–8 PM, $5 cover (12 and under free). Upcoming dates are always listed above.
How do I get a gym badge?
Badges are earned from participating vendors — the "Gym Leaders" — and each Leader sets their own challenge to earn theirs. Badges are serialized and limited, so they're first-come while they last. Get each badge validated at the show to lock in your entry.
What's the grand prize?
At Vermillion City, collecting badges earns entries toward a $1,000 grand-prize shopping spree, with the winner drawn Saturday at 8 PM (or once all badges are claimed). Prize details are set per event.
What can I buy, sell, and trade?
The whole hobby — Pokémon, Premium One Piece, and sports cards, plus whatever the Maui vendor community brings to trade.
