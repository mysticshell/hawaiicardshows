About Keep It Aloha

Keep It Aloha is a recurring trading card show in Honolulu hosted by Kamaka Dias and Hawaii Collectibles. The series debuted May 2–3, 2026 at SALT at Our Kaka'ako with 70+ vendors and more than 5,000 attendees across two days.

The inaugural show was free admission for the public, made possible through partnerships with @getcollectr, @waiakea, Aloha Card Shop, and @merchlabs. Each upcoming show may have its own admission and vendor details — check the next-show details above for what's confirmed.

The vibe leans festival, not transactional — live music, families, first-time collectors, longtime vendors, all under the SALT awnings. As Kamaka puts it: "It's truly a kākou thing."

FAQ

Is Keep It Aloha really free?
Admission has been free at past Keep It Aloha shows — no door charge for the public.* *Details are subject to change between shows. Always check the upcoming-show details above for the most current admission policy.
Where is the show held?
For 2026, all Keep It Aloha shows are being held at SALT at Our Kaka'ako (691 Auahi St, Honolulu). Beyond 2026, the venue may change — check the upcoming-show details above for the confirmed venue per date.
What kinds of cards are at the show?
Mixed TCG and sports — Pokemon, One Piece, Dragon Ball, Yu-Gi-Oh, Japanese imports, and modern + vintage sports. The vendor mix is intentionally broad.
How do I become a vendor?
Reach out to @keepitalohaevents or @hnlcollectibles on Instagram to apply for an upcoming date.
Are kids welcome?
Absolutely — Keep It Aloha is family-friendly. The first show was full of keiki, strollers, and first-time collectors.
