About the Show

Held at the Ala Moana Hotel in the heart of Honolulu, this is two collector events running side by side — the long-running Hawaii Diecast & Collectibles Show in the Hibiscus Ballroom and The Card District in the Garden Ballroom. Walk the diecast, comics, and action-figure tables, then cross the hall for Pokémon, One Piece, sports cards, and sealed product.

It's a great one for the whole family — kids 10 and under get in free, there's a huge range of price points across 100+ combined tables, and the hotel has easy parking and food on site.

FAQ

How much is admission?
$6 gets you into both shows — the Hawaii Diecast & Collectibles Show and The Card District. Children 10 and under are free.
Where is it?
The Ala Moana Hotel, 410 Atkinson Dr, Honolulu, HI 96814. The Diecast show is in the Hibiscus Ballroom and The Card District is in the Garden Ballroom.
What will I find?
Diecast cars, comic books, and action figures on the collectibles side; Pokémon, One Piece, sports cards, and more on the card side — 100+ dealer tables combined.
How do I become a vendor?
Email PonoPromotions@gmail.com or reach out via the Hawaii Die Cast Show page on Facebook.
