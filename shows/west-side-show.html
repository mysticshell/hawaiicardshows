About West Side Show

West Side Show is a two-day Pokemon-focused trading card show on the west side of Oahu, hosted at the Kroc Center in Kapolei / Ewa Beach. It's organized by Rocket Relics — the same crew behind Mini Pokemon Market and The Card District.

It runs twice in 2026 — once in June and again in December — giving collectors on the west side a major show they don't have to drive across the island for. Expect a mix of singles, sealed product, slabs, Japanese imports, and trade activity across both days.

The Kroc Center's Ballrooms & Lanai give the show plenty of room for vendor tables plus an outdoor lanai, which makes for a different feel than the indoor-only shows in town.

FAQ

When and where is the next West Side Show?
See the "Next Show" callout at the top of this page — it pulls the next upcoming date directly from our event calendar. The 2026 schedule is West Side Show III on June 6–7 and West Side Show IV on December 5–6, both at the Kroc Center in Kapolei.
Where exactly is the Kroc Center?
91-3257 Kualakai Pkwy, Ewa Beach, HI 96706. It's on the west side of Oahu, about 25 minutes from Honolulu depending on traffic. The show uses the Kroc Center's Ballrooms & Lanai space.
What kinds of cards are at the show?
Pokemon is the primary focus — sealed product, singles, slabs, and Japanese imports — though many vendors carry sports cards and other TCGs too. The vendor mix can shift between editions.
How do I become a vendor?
DM @rocketrelics on Instagram to apply for vendor space for an upcoming edition.
Is there parking?
Yes — the Kroc Center has on-site parking. Lot fills up faster during the busiest middle hours, so arriving earlier or later helps if you need a closer spot.
Are kids welcome?
Yes — West Side Show is family-friendly, and the Kroc Center is a community venue. Bring the keiki.
