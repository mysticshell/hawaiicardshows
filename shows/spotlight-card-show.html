About the Spotlight Card Show in Hawaii

The Spotlight Card Show is a traveling card show brand — hosted on Instagram as @spotlightcardshow — that runs large-scale, production-heavy shows built around the whole hobby. When Spotlight comes to Honolulu, it takes over marquee O‘ahu venues like the Hawaii Convention Center and Blaisdell Arena, bringing a vendor floor and crowd on a scale Hawaii rarely sees.

The floor spans the full hobby: modern and vintage sports cards, Pokemon, and TCG, alongside breakers, grading, and collectibles. If you follow Spotlight from the mainland and want to know when they're back in the islands, this page tracks every confirmed Hawaii date — with hours, venue, and tickets — as it's announced.

Tickets & Admission

Tickets are sold through Spotlight directly. Buy online in advance for the best price — walk-up admission is typically higher at the door.

  • Presale tickets — available online at spotlightcardshow.com ahead of each show.
  • Free admission for active & retired military (with valid ID) at past Hawaii editions.
  • Free admission for kids under 13 when accompanied by an adult.

Current pricing and any perk changes are always confirmed on Spotlight's ticket page for the specific date.

Vendor Information

Vendor tables are offered for each Hawaii show. Packages have included:

  • 6' fully covered tables with 2 chairs
  • Marketing / promotion for your table
  • 2 vendor badges per table

To ask about table availability for an upcoming Hawaii date, reach out to @spotlightcardshow on Instagram or through spotlightcardshow.com.
