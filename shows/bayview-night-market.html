About Bayview Night Market

The Bayview Night Market trade night is a recurring TCG meet hosted by @pkmn.collective, happening alongside the regular @bayviewnightmarket at Bayview Golf Course in Kaneohe. Every 1st and 3rd Tuesday of the month — buy, sell, trade, and vibe on the East Side.

It's the rare Hawaii card show that doesn't make Windward and East Side collectors drive into town. Co-located with a working night market means food trucks, drinks, and a built-in foot-traffic crowd that isn't only there for the cards.

FAQ

When is the next Bayview Night Market trade night?
It runs every 1st and 3rd Tuesday of the month. The next confirmed date is shown above — all upcoming dates are listed in the Upcoming Dates section.
Where exactly is the venue?
Bayview Golf Course, 45-285 Kaneohe Bay Dr, Kaneohe, HI 96744 — on the Windward / East Side of Oahu, about 30 minutes from Honolulu.
What can I buy, sell, or trade?
Pokémon, One Piece, sports, and other TCG — sealed product, singles, slabs, and crafts. The night market itself adds food trucks, drinks, and other vendors.
Is it free to attend?
Yes — attending the trade night and the broader night market is free.
How do I become a vendor?
Reach out to @pkmn.collective on Instagram. They handle vendor coordination for the trade-night portion.
Is parking available?
Yes — Bayview Golf Course has its own parking lot and the night market crowd shares the space.
