About the Space 62 Collectibles Show

Space 62 is a collectibles destination in the Ala Moana Annex (Upper Level) — next to Buffalo Wild Wings & Target — and its multi-day collectibles shows have quickly become one of the most central, easy-to-reach card events on Oʻahu. Expect around 60 vendors, raffle prizes all day, a panel, and intro classes — a free Friday trade night kicks things off, then full Saturday and Sunday show days.

Being right inside Ala Moana means no separate venue hunt: covered parking, food court and restaurants, and steady mall foot traffic are all built in. It's an easy one to bring family along to, or to swing by between other stops.

FAQ

When is the next Space 62 show?+
The next confirmed date is shown above, and all upcoming dates are listed in the Upcoming Dates section — pulled live from our calendar.
Where is Space 62?+
In the Ala Moana Annex (Upper Level), next to Buffalo Wild Wings & Target — 1450 Ala Moana Blvd, Honolulu, HI 96814. Centrally located with covered mall parking and food on site.
How much is admission?+
Friday's Trade Night is free. Saturday and Sunday show days are $5 — or bring a can of non-perishable food instead. All proceeds go to the Hawaii Foodbank.
What can I buy, sell, or trade?+
Pokémon, sports, One Piece, and other TCG — sealed product, singles, and slabs, across around 60 vendors on show days, plus raffle prizes, a panel, and intro classes. Friday kicks off with a free trade night.
Is there parking?+
Yes — Ala Moana Center has large covered parking structures, plus the full food court and restaurants if you want to make an afternoon of it.
How do I become a vendor?+
Reach out to @space62honolulu on Instagram — they coordinate vendor tables for each show.
