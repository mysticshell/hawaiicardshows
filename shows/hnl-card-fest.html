About HNL Card Fest

HNL Card Fest is a two-day cards and collectibles festival held at the Hawaii Convention Center in Honolulu, hosted by @hnlcardfest. It's one of the few Hawaii card events to take over a full convention-center hall — a scale that lets it bring together vendors, live trading, cosplay, and family-friendly programming all under one roof.

The format spans the whole hobby: modern and vintage sports cards, Pokemon, and TCG alongside collectibles, with room for the kind of crowd a convention center is built for. The hosts pitch it plainly — a show "vendors aren't going to wanna miss," built for the whole family to enjoy.

The festival runs as a recurring summer event, with editions booked at the Convention Center for both 2026 and 2027. Confirmed dates, hours, and the hall for each edition are listed below.

FAQ

When and where is HNL Card Fest?+
HNL Card Fest is held at the Hawaii Convention Center (1801 Kalakaua Ave, Honolulu) as a two-day summer festival, 9 AM – 8 PM each day. The next confirmed dates and the exact hall are listed in the upcoming-editions section above.
What kinds of cards and collectibles are there?+
The whole hobby — modern and vintage sports cards, Pokemon, and TCG, plus collectibles. The convention-center footprint means a deep, varied vendor floor.
Is it family-friendly?+
Yes — HNL Card Fest is built for families and kids, with cosplay and family-friendly programming alongside the vendor floor.
How do I become a vendor?+
Reach out to @hnlcardfest on Instagram to ask about table availability for an upcoming edition.
