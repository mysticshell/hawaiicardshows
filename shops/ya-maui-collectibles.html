About the Shop

Maui's Spot for Cards & Cute Finds

Ya Maui Collectibles is a Wailuku shop blending trading cards with custom gifts, collectibles, and cute accessories. Located on Main Street in the heart of Wailuku, it's a welcoming spot for Maui collectors and TCG fans — whether you're hunting for your next pull or picking up a one-of-a-kind gift.

Beyond the shop, Ya Maui Collectibles hosts the Ya Maui Collectibles Card Show — an evening of buying, selling, trading, and connecting with fellow collectors from across Maui. It's become a go-to gathering for the island's growing card community.

Follow @ya_maui_collectibles on Instagram for current open days, new arrivals, and card show announcements.
