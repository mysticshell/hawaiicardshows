About the Shop

Two Locations, One Massive Selection

Hi Collector operates out of two Oahu locations, making it one of the more accessible collectibles shops on the island. Their Pearlridge Center kiosk is open daily during mall hours, while the main Dillingham Blvd store is open Saturdays from 11 AM to 4 PM.

The shop carries a broad range of collectibles that goes well beyond cards. From Pokemon and sports cards to comics, Star Wars figures, Godzilla items, G.I. Joe, and die-cast cars, Hi Collector caters to collectors across multiple categories. They also buy collections, so if you're looking to sell, they're worth a visit.

Whether you're browsing the Pearlridge kiosk on a weekday or making the trip to the Dillingham store on Saturday for the full experience, Hi Collector has something for every type of collector.
