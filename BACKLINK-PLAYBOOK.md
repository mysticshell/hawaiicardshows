# External Backlink Playbook

Practical workflow for earning backlinks to `hawaiicardshows.com`. Built for Tyler to execute solo, no marketing-tool subscriptions required.

**Goal:** 5-10 quality backlinks from relevant sources within 60 days. Each one moves SEO ranking velocity for the pillar pages. Quality and relevance beat volume — one Beckett link is worth 100 spammy ones.

---

## Phase 1 — Quick Wins (this week, ~1 hour total)

These are the lowest-friction, highest-relevance backlinks. Start here.

### 1. Ask organizers + vendors to link their series page from their Linktree

This is the easiest win in the playbook. These people already know you, already trust the site, and most have a Linktree or website where they list relevant links anyway. Adding a one-line URL is a 30-second favor.

**Who to ask, what to ask for:**

| Person / Org | DM | What to send them |
|---|---|---|
| Kamaka (Hawaii Collectibles) | @kamakarips | `hawaiicardshows.com/shows/keep-it-aloha` |
| Javin (Paradise Card Show) | @paradisecards_ | `hawaiicardshows.com/shows/paradise-card-show` |
| PKMN Collective | @pkmn.collective | `hawaiicardshows.com/shows/bayview-night-market` |
| Maui Sports Cards | @mauisportscards | `hawaiicardshows.com/` (or once we have a series page) |
| Bubbah's Toy Box | (DM via shop) | `hawaiicardshows.com/` |
| GetNutz | @getnutzwearhawaii | `hawaiicardshows.com/` |
| ToyLynx | (shop website) | Their shop page `hawaiicardshows.com/shops/toylynx` |
| Aloha Card Shop | (any of 3 locations) | Their shop page `hawaiicardshows.com/shops/aloha-card-shop` |

**Copy-paste DM script:**

> Hey [name] — quick favor. We just upgraded the [their show] page on Hawaii Card Shows so it auto-pulls all upcoming dates, has the venue, schedule, photos, etc. If you have a Linktree or links on your site, would you mind adding it? Just helps people find your show easier and helps the calendar grow. 🤙
>
> Link: `hawaiicardshows.com/shows/[their-show-slug]`

**Why this works:** You're not asking for promotion — you're handing them a useful tool that benefits their attendees. Most will say yes within a day.

---

### 2. Add the press page link to your email signature + Instagram bio

If anyone hits your IG bio looking for press info, give them a one-click path:

- **Instagram bio:** add a line like "Press kit: hawaiicardshows.com/press"
- **Email signature:** include `hawaiicardshows.com/press` underneath your contact info

Cumulative effect: every email you send is a passive backlink opportunity.

## Phase 4 — Community Backlinks (ongoing, low-effort)

Easy passive plays:

- **Reddit:** post once in r/sportscards and once in r/PokemonTCG introducing the site. Don't spam — one good post per sub. Make it useful (e.g., "If you're flying to Hawaii, here's the card show calendar").
- **Discord servers:** Hawaii-specific Pokemon/sports Discords. Drop the link in #resources channels.
- **Facebook groups:** Hawaii TCG groups, Hawaii card collectors groups. Same rule — useful first post, not a sales pitch.
- **Comment on hobby YouTube:** if a relevant video drops about regional scenes or growth, drop a thoughtful comment with a link.

## Tracking — keep it simple

Open a single Google Doc or spreadsheet. Columns:

| Outlet | Contact | Pitch Date | Angle | Response | Follow-Up Date | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Don't over-build this. Even 20 entries tracked properly beats a fancy CRM that nobody opens.

---

## Cadence

- **3-5 pitches per week.** Quality + research > volume.
- **One follow-up per pitch, 7 days later.** Then move on.
- **Track everything**, even the no-responses. Patterns emerge.
- **Re-pitch successful angles to new outlets** — if "founder story" lands once, lead with it again.

---

## What success looks like

After 60 days you should have:
- 3-5 vendor/organizer Linktree backlinks (Phase 1)
- 1-2 hobby press features or podcast appearances
- 1-2 Hawaii local press mentions
- Steady community presence on Reddit/Discord/Facebook groups

Each backlink should be tracked in the sheet so we can measure GA4 referral traffic against effort.

---

## When to revisit

After every 60 days, audit:
- Which angles landed? Lean into those.
- Which outlets ignored? Drop or change angles.
- Which backlinks drove the most GA4 traffic? Pursue similar outlets.
- Build the next batch of targets from there.
