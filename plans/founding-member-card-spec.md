# HCS Founding Member Card — Design Spec (Phase 2, banked)

> **Status:** Banked for Phase 2. Decision (2026-06-11): launch the referral-weighted
> newsletter giveaway *first*, mint founding cards *later* once referral data shows who the
> real ambassadors are. This spec is the ready-to-execute design when we pull the trigger.

## Concept
A matte-black steel trading card, the emerald HCS "H" etched in silver, each one carrying its
owner's **name and number** — so it isn't "a Hawaii Card Shows card," it's *their* card,
`No. 007 of 150`. The reward that makes all founding subscribers feel like owners → evangelists.

## Vendor & verified pricing
- **My Metal Business Card — Metal Trading Cards** (https://mymetalbusinesscard.com/product/metal-trading-cards/)
- 0.5mm stainless steel, 3.5″ × 2.5″ (standard card size)
- Per-card: 100 = $3.02 · 150 = $2.78 · 200 = $2.70 · 250 = $2.53
- 1 free screen-print color (front+back), free silver laser etching, **free "Variable Data" serialization** (name + number via spreadsheet upload), free protective sleeve, no setup fee with vector art ($60 if design needed), ~2–3 wk turnaround
- **All-in ~150 units: ~$480–$550** · ~250 units: ~$700–$780 (before one-touch cases ~$1–2 ea)

## Material & finish
- **Matte Black** finish (best "limited/chase" read + silver-etch contrast). Gunmetal is backup.
- **Silver laser etching** = primary "ink" (engraved, permanent, premium)
- **1 free screen-print color = gold `#ffc625`** as the single pop (black+silver+gold = "rare")
- Optional 2nd print color = emerald `#1a6b5a` (small upcharge — decide on proof)

## Front (hero)
- Large silver-etched **"H" mark**, upper-center
- **HAWAII CARD SHOWS** wordmark beneath, Phudu, screen-printed gold
- Bottom third, big: **`FOUNDING MEMBER · No. 007 / 150`** (serial in gold)
- Faint etched **island-chain silhouette** along bottom edge (guilloché-style collectible motif)

## Back (story + tie-in)
- Founder's creed, etched, HCS voice:
  > *You were here first — before the crowds, before the hype. You believed Hawaii's collectors
  > deserved a home. This card says so. One of 150. So are you.* — **The HCS Team**
- `Founding Members Series · Est. 2026`
- **Member's name** etched per card (variable data — the irreplaceable, un-resellable detail)
- **Laser-etched QR code** → "Founding Members" registry page on hawaiicardshows.com (digital
  Founders Wall — scan card, see your name among the founders)

## Serialization mechanic
"Variable Data" pulls **name + number** per card from one spreadsheet → personalization +
serialization at $0 extra. `#001–114` reserved for current founders; **higher numbers earned
through referrals** — the card doubles as the referral-ladder trophy.

## Presentation
Ship in a magnetic "one-touch" style case + custom HCS-labeled insert + numbered COA card, in a
slim black mailer. Speaks collectors' language (slabs/one-touches).

## Open decisions when we activate
- Run size & top number: **lean 250** (headroom for referral milestones; barely moves per-unit)
- 1 color (gold-only) vs 2 (gold+emerald) — decide on proof; start gold-only
