## The Post

Quick life update — it's been a minute since I've been active here.

Short version of where the last few years went: after my plans for a career in international education got rerouted by COVID, I spent five years building digital community products for founders across SE Asia, Australia, and the Philippines. Web3, gaming, AI — the whole tour.

But the more time I spent building communities online, the more I noticed people craving the opposite: actual in-person gathering. So I came back to that in the most unexpected way possible.

I went to a card show at a golf course in Hawaii. Got hooked. Spent the next year vending shows, helping organize events at golf courses and music venues, and getting to know every card shop on the island.

Whole time I kept running into the same problem: finding card shows here was nearly impossible. Instagram-only. Group-chat-only. Sometimes nothing at all.

So I built **Hawaii Card Shows** — the free community calendar for trading card events across the islands. Started as a personal rolodex, turned into a two-week AI-assisted build, and now:

→ 7,500+ unique visitors
→ 27,000+ pageviews
→ 77 events tracked across all four Hawaiian islands
→ 72% of traffic from organic Google search
→ 120+ newsletter subscribers

Zero paid marketing. Two and a half months in. All organic, all word of mouth, all SEO.

The full story of how international ed → web3 → Hawaii card shows fits together is genuinely wild and I'll write that one up properly soon. For now — if you're in the card show space, building community, or just curious how community moves from digital to physical, let's connect.

🔗 hawaiicardshows.com
