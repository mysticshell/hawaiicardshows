This whole thing started because we were tired of answering one question: when's the next card show? 🃏

In a single month we've come across just about every kind of show you can imagine — six vendors in a boba shop, a hundred vendors and Max Holloway taking the scene by storm. The site keeps growing, and we'll keep making it better.

But here's the thing: we can't be everywhere at once. You can.

Got an epic coin flip? An insane pack rip? A wholesome deal or trade that made your day? Send it to us. We'll do our best to feature as many as we can in our show recaps — and build an archive of these moments on each show's page so they live on. 🌺

📲 How to send it in:
→ DM us @hawaiicardshows
→ Tag us in your clip
→ Or email aloha@hawaiicardshows.com

And a huge shoutout to @sarukofamcollects for always showing up and posting walkthroughs of every show — you're the blueprint. 🎥🙏
