Oahu just had its biggest week of card shows. 🤙🌺

It started Tuesday on the East Side… and ended with a UFC champ behind a vendor table. Swipe through ➡️

🟣 TUE — Bayview Night Market (Kaneohe)
The week kicked off with @pkmn.collective's trade night at Bayview Golf Course. Pokemon, One Piece, sports, slabs, ono food under the lights.

🟢 FRI — Keep It Aloha #2 (SALT Kaka'ako)
70+ vendors, 1,500+ collectors — free for everyone, on a single pau-hana Friday night. The festival came back, and it felt like a night out with friends. Mahalo @kamakarips. (Full recap live now — link in bio.)

🟠 SAT–SUN — West Side Card Show III (Ewa Beach)
Then it exploded. @rocket_relics brought Max Holloway out for his vendor debut — lines out the door to the street, vibes immaculate. Waianae's own talking cards with the West Side. You can't script it better.

Three shows. One week. One community. 🏝️
