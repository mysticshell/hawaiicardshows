Hawaii just had its biggest week of card shows. 🤙🌺

It started Tuesday on the East Side… and ended with a UFC champ behind a vendor table. Swipe through ➡️

🟣 TUE — Bayview Night Market (Kaneohe)
The week kicked off with @pkmn.collective's trade night at Bayview Golf Course. Pokemon, One Piece, sports, slabs, ono food under the lights.

🟢 FRI — Keep It Aloha #2 (SALT Kaka'ako)
The festival came back for a pau-hana Friday night — still free for the public, all ages. Mahalo @kamakarips.

🟠 SAT–SUN — West Side Show III (Ewa Beach)
Then it exploded. @rocketrelics brought Max Holloway out for his vendor debut — lines out the door to the street, vibes immaculate. Waianae's own talking cards with the West Side. You can't script it better.

Three shows. One week. One community. 🏝️
