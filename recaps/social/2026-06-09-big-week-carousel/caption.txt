Hawaii just had one of its biggest weeks of card shows — capped by Max Holloway's vending debut. 🤙🌺

In one week, the whole energy of Hawaii's card scene got captured across three shows. Swipe through ➡️

🟣 TUE — Bayview Night Market (Kaneohe)
A lively Tuesday night market to kick it off — casual deals, food trucks, and ono grinds at a local golf course. Bayview's become a staple of the East Side. Mahalo @pkmn.collective.

🟢 FRI — Keep It Aloha #2 (SALT Kaka'ako)
Friday-night pau hana vibes under the lights, in the heart of a buzzing Kaka'ako. 70+ vendors, 1,500+ collectors — free for everyone. @kamakarips and crew are blazing a new path in town. Mahalo @kamakarips, @hnlcollectibles + @hicollectibles. (Recap live — link in bio.)

🟠 SAT–SUN — West Side Card Show III (Ewa Beach)
"Hooo, you frickin' guy, dawg!" @rocket_relics brought Max Holloway out for his vendor debut — 100+ vendors, 9,000+ collectors over two days, lines out the door to the street. The West Side might just be the Best Side. Mahalo @rocket_relics, @ditzypulls + @fromthehearthawaii. (Recap live — link in bio.)

A lively East Side night market, a Friday pau-hana show in town, and a massive weekend out West. One week, one community. 🏝️
