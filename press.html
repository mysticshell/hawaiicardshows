The Quick Pitch

For journalists and podcasters who need a 30-second summary.

  • What it is
    A free, community-driven calendar of every trading card show, trade night, and TCG event across the Hawaiian Islands.
  • Who it serves
    Collectors, vendors, organizers, and shops across Pokemon, sports cards, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh, Magic, and Japanese imports — on Oahu, Maui, the Big Island, and Kauai.
  • Why it exists
    Before this site, finding a Hawaii card show meant scrolling Instagram and asking around. Hawaii Card Shows is the first single source of truth.

    March 2026

Boilerplate & Suggested Copy

Drop-in text for articles, podcast intros, or partnership announcements.

Short (1 sentence)

Hawaii Card Shows is the free community calendar for every trading card show, trade night, and TCG event across the Hawaiian Islands.

Medium (2-3 sentences)

Hawaii Card Shows is a community-driven calendar tracking every confirmed card show, trade night, and TCG event across Oahu, Maui, the Big Island, and Kauai. Founded in 2026 by a Hawaii-based collector, the site is free to use and free to list, and now reaches 2,000+ monthly visitors — 79% of them in the islands.

Founder Quote
