Note from the HCS Team

A brand-new show set up right on the Blaisdell Arena floor — the room that usually hosts concerts and games — and packed it out through the hottest weekend of the year. Spotlight is a Southern California show planting its first flag in Hawaii, and it’s fun to watch the islands land on a bigger map. Mahalo to the Spotlight crew for bringing it over.

Instead of the usual Exhibition Hall, Spotlight set up on the floor of the Arena itself for its June 20–21 debut. 100+ vendors, collectors in from the neighbor islands, the mainland, and even overseas, and one now-infamous 30-pound metal Mew binder making the rounds. Two days, two different energies — a packed Saturday and an easier Sunday.
