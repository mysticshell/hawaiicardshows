The Kickoff · Tue June 2 · Kaneohe

It started at Bayview

The week opened on the East Side. @pkmn.collective’s Tuesday trade night at Bayview Golf Course set the tone — Pokemon, One Piece, sports, slabs, and ono food under the lights. The steady East Side heartbeat that keeps the scene going between the big shows.

Keep It Aloha came back for a Friday night

The Finale · Sat–Sun June 6–7 · Ewa Beach

Then Max Holloway broke the internet

The week ended with a bang. Waianae’s own — a former UFC featherweight champion — made his vendor debut at West Side Show III. Lines ran out the door to the street, the vibes were immaculate, and collectors got to talk cards with a hometown legend. Hosted by @rocketrelics.
