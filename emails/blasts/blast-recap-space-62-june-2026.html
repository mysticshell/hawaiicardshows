Note from the HCS Team

Space 62 is best known for virtual reality — but their first Collectibles Show made the case that they’re about all forms of entertainment. In a year where shows keep getting overrun, they nailed the thing collectors have been missing: room to roam. A strong debut, and a good sign for where the scene is headed. Mahalo to the Space 62 crew.

Fun fact we didn’t know until this weekend: outer space officially starts about 62 miles up. Fitting — because the thing Space 62 nailed was space, the physical kind. Their first show pulled 4,800+ collectors across three days and still felt navigable, with $10K+ in raffles, live quiz shows, cosplay, and local artist Anh Vu drawing on-site. Admission was $5 or a can of food, and the community filled 40+ boxes for the Hawaii Food Bank.
